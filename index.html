Privacy & transparency

SHAHEEN's browser-based tools are designed to process your input locally whenever practical. Saved notes, favourites and theme settings may use local browser storage. Clearing browser data can remove them. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and your region's consent requirements.

Do not upload confidential documents or personal information unless you understand how the feature processes it. For an actual public launch, replace this notice with a complete, accurate privacy policy and contact details for the site owner.
