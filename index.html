About this project

SHAHEEN

Developer label: Shaheen Tools Team

Purpose

SHAHEEN combines practical browser-based utilities with study, planning, focus and wellbeing features. The goal is to save people time and help them complete real tasks.

Data and privacy

Saved tools, tasks, notes, habits, theme and custom logo are kept in your browser's local storage. They do not automatically sync across devices. Do not save passwords or confidential information here.

The QR generator sends the entered text to an external QR service to generate the image. Image optimization happens locally in your browser. Monetag ad scripts may process information under the ad provider's policies. Review applicable privacy and consent requirements before public launch.

Ads

Advertisements are independent from tool controls. We do not ask visitors to click ads, and tools should never be disguised as ads.

Google products

Google Search Console monitors a website's search presence. Google Play Console manages Android apps; a website cannot register an Android app listing by adding SEO tags alone.
